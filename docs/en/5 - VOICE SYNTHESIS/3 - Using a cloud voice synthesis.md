
**IMPORTANT: We recommend keeping the Windows voice synthesis enabled in case of network problems. See [[2 - Windows voice synthesis]]**

Activate the extension corresponding to your subscription (Elevenlabs or Speechify), from:

*Settings > Home > Extensions > TTS*

**NOTE**: **If you enable more than one cloud provider, your voice will be doubled.**
**You must restart IGOOR when you enable / disable a new extension.**

You will then need to **enter your API key** to access the service. For example, for Speechify:

![[../assets/speechify.png]]

**Once the API key is entered, the list of available voices will be filled in automatically.**

Then, depending on your subscription, you will be able to choose a specific voice (your cloned voice, or another voice) and customize a few parameters such as pitch, speed, and volume:

![[test_voice.png]]

By clicking *Test voice*, you can verify that your adjustments are going in the right direction.
