## Benefits
**Cloning your voice has obvious benefits for the user and for those around them: the voice helps maintain the emotional connection with the person who has lost it.**

## Instant cloning

It is possible to clone your voice directly from IGOOR.
You can do this in two ways:

1) From the audio collected through the [[2 - Recording the biographical memory]]
2) From an audio file between 1' and 3' in length

### Voice cloning from biographical memory recordings

Go to:

*Home > Voice synthesis > Configure Elevenlabs (or Speechify) voice*

On the extension page, click on:

*Use recorded voice*

![[voice_cloning.png]]

This will create a new voice from your audio recordings.

**NOTE: If this button is grayed out, it means IGOOR does not have recordings to clone your voice.** 

The clone will then be automatically selected in the voice dropdown menu, and it will appear with a name like "Voice Clone Jul 24" (voice clone performed on July 24).

### Voice cloning from an audio file

A user who no longer has access to their voice can still clone it from previous recordings. We can assist you in creating this audio file.

## Professional cloning

**Professional** cloning can only be done through the Elevenlabs platform.
It requires at least half an hour, and ideally up to **three hours, of professional voice recordings.**

Although longer and more "bureaucratic" (Elevenlabs asks for documents that certify your identity), a well-executed professional cloning guarantees a quality, variety, and richness of the voice clone superior to instant cloning.

Read this [complete guide for cloning via Elevenlabs](https://elevenlabs.io/fr/blog/voice-cloning-deep-dive). 

Once the cloning is done on Elevenlabs, simply choose the clone from the dropdown menu of available voices.
