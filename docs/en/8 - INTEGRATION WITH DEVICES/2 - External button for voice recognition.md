Any external button compatible with your device can be used to trigger voice recognition.
The button just needs to send a keyboard shortcut to IGOOR.
**The default keyboard shortcut is CTRL+K**, but you can change it at any time in:

*Settings > Home > Voice recognition > Configure voice recognition*

Click on the text field:

![[../assets/keyboard_combination.png]]

And click your external button. The key combination sent by your external button will appear in the text field.
Save the settings.
