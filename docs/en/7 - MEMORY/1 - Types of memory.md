
Starting from version 1, you can give IGOOR plenty of information from the very beginning, through the [[2 - Recording the biographical memory]].

IGOOR is equipped with three types of memory:

- [[3 - Memory extracted from your documents]] (manual, static)
- [[4 - Long term memory]] (automatic, incremental)
- [[5 - Short term memory]] (automatic, dynamic)

These memories are used in different ways to personalize the software's predictions:

- the first one is provided by you;
- the other two are automatically added by the AI based on your conversations through the IGOOR software.
