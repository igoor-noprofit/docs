
The long term memory gathers all the information about you or your loved ones that are facts or relationships or stable preferences.
For example:

> Pierre loves Tarantino's cinema

is a stable preference.

> Pierre went to see a Tarantino film this afternoon

on the other hand, is information for the short term memory.

Facts, opinions, preferences, etc. must be validated by the AI before they can be integrated into the long term memory.
