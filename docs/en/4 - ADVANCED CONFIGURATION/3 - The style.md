Although not mandatory, it is important to fill in this field with indications about the style of the user's sentences, which can deeply change the predictions generated by IGOOR. E.g.:

> She expresses herself with long sentences, with plenty of philosophical quotes and irony

will yield very different predictions from:

> He likes to use short, concise sentences, with a very informal tone

You can customize the style in:

*Settings > Bio*

The style also depends on past conversations, and in particular on past conversations with the current interlocutor, etc.
