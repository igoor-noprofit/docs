
## Experimental status notice

Thank you for your interest in our application. Please note that, although it has reached version 1, IGOOR is **experimental software**.

**What this means:**

- **Bugs and instability:** Like any application, IGOOR may contain errors and bugs, and may crash unexpectedly.

- **Incomplete features:** Some features may be missing, incomplete, undocumented, or may not work as expected. They are liable to be changed or removed in future versions. We make every effort to keep this documentation up to date and as complete as possible.

- **Possible incompatibilities with future versions:** We make every effort to guarantee compatibility between versions. However, using this application may not be fully compatible with upcoming versions of the IGOOR software. You can export/import your data at any time from *Settings > Home*.
